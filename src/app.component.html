Bring a song

Paste a YouTube or Spotify track link. When available, we find a matching 30-second audio preview. Or upload your own audio, analyzed privately in your browser.

02

Find the musical fingerprint

We estimate the key and chord changes from the audio’s pitch content. The first 90 seconds of uploads are analyzed. Tempo and meter are shown only in the curated demo.

03

Make it yours

Click chords, loop the synth progression, change instruments, or transpose to a key that suits you. Save your discoveries locally or export a chord sheet.

Streaming links don’t grant full audio access. Preview matches may be different versions. Automated key and chord estimates can be imperfect, especially for complex recordings.
